THE LUMEN ARCHIVE · NIGHT SHIFT

SHADOWLINE

8 Galleries · 24 Guards · 38 Shards · 1 Perfect Heist

You are Moth, a courier-thief. The museum sleeps, the star-shards glow, and twenty-four guards walk their rounds. Slip between the cones of light, steal every shard, and reach the exit without ever being seen.

An original game. No network. Sound is synthesized live in your browser.

CHOOSE A GALLERY

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D / ARROWS

Move Moth through the galleries. Walls, crates, display cases and tall plants block movement — and block a guard's sight.

SHIFT — SNEAK

Move slowly and quietly. Your detection radius shrinks and your footsteps go silent. Patience is the whole job.

SPACE — DASH

A quick roll to cross open floor. Fast, but loud: guards hear it and turn to look. Cooldown 1.2s.

CLICK — THROW COIN

Toss a coin to a floor tile. It clinks, and the nearest guard walks over to investigate. 3 coins per gallery.

VISION CONES

A guard's cone is what he can see. Yellow means he is growing suspicious. Red means he is hunting you. Break line of sight and hide in shadow until his meter drains.

SHADOW & RANK

Standing in dark shadow shrinks the distance a guard can spot you. Finish unseen with every shard for a GHOST rank; get spotted and the best you can earn is SEEN.
